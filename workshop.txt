title=[42.22.2] Improvised Weapon XP
description=In Build 42, melee weapons whose only category is [b]Improvised[/b] (pens, pencils, the Geometry Compass, stones and flint nodules) give Strength and Fitness XP, but no weapon skill XP at all. This mod lets them train a weapon skill.
description=
description=[b]Only XP changes. Damage, critical hits and hit chance stay as in vanilla.[/b]
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Stabbing weapons → Short Blade:[/b] all pens and pencils, Geometry Compass
description=[*][b]Swinging weapons → Short Blunt:[/b] Stone, Flint Nodule
description=[*][b]Same amount as vanilla:[/b] damage × 0.9 per hit, at most 3, before the XP multiplier, the same as a weapon with a skill category
description=[*][b]No double XP:[/b] weapons that already train a skill are not touched
description=[*][b]Modded weapons:[/b] the rule goes by category, not by item name, so modded melee weapons without a skill category follow it too (stab → Short Blade, spear → Spear, swing → Short Blunt)
description=[*]Bare hands stay as they are (Build 42 has no unarmed skill). Thrown bombs, traps and firecrackers are not melee and are left alone.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Adds a handler to vanilla's weapon hit XP event. No vanilla file is replaced.
description=[*]Multiplayer: add the mod to the server. Not tested in multiplayer.
description=[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
description=[/list]
